Privacy Policy – Mystery Tile Sort

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Overview

Mystery Tile Sort ("the app") is a puzzle game. We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address, or other personal information ourselves.

Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See Google's advertising policies and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app shows a consent form before personalised ads are served. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Google settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google AdMob, as described above.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
